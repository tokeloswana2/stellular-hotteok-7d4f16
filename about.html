About Councillors Hub

Our Story

Driving better local governance through knowledge, strategy and accountability.

Councillors Hub is a 100% black-owned think tank specialising in municipal governance, strategy development and capacity building. We support municipalities and councillors with research-led insight that strengthens service delivery, oversight and public trust.

Vision

To become a leading hub for research, learning and knowledge sharing that empowers municipalities and councillors.

Mission

To deliver research, data analytics, strategic advisory and capacity-building services that empower municipalities and councillors to make informed decisions, strengthen oversight, and improve public value.
